# TechNova Electronics -- Shipping Policy

> **Project note:** TechNova Electronics is a fictional company. This
> document adapts the supplied shipping information for the TechNova
> project.

## Orders

-   Amazon ships every TechNova customer order.
-   Delivery dates and tracking information are available in **Your
    Orders** on Amazon.
-   TechNova cannot view or change an Amazon order's delivery
    arrangements.
-   For questions about an order's delivery date or tracking status,
    direct the customer to **Amazon → Your Orders**.

## Replacement Parts

Replacement parts and accessory kits are shipped from TechNova's own
warehouse.

-   Replacement parts and kits are provided free of charge.
-   Orders leave the warehouse within **2 business days**.
-   Delivery usually takes **3 to 7 business days** after dispatch.
-   The customer receives a tracking link by email when the parcel
    leaves the warehouse.

Do not tell the customer that a replacement part has shipped until
dispatch has been confirmed. The tracking link is sent by email when the
parcel leaves the warehouse.

## Countries and Delivery Address

-   TechNova sells in the countries where its products are listed on
    Amazon.
-   Replacement parts are shipped to the same address as the original
    order.
-   If the customer needs a replacement part sent to a different
    address, refer the request to a human colleague rather than
    promising an address change.
