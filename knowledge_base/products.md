# TechNova Electronics -- Product Knowledge Base

## 1. Wireless Headphones

### Product Models

-   **TN-H100 -- Essential Wireless Headphones:** Bluetooth 5.2, up to
    20 hours of battery life, built-in microphone, lightweight over-ear
    design, playback controls. Suitable for everyday listening and
    online classes.
-   **TN-H200 -- Premium Wireless Headphones:** Bluetooth 5.3, Active
    Noise Cancellation (ANC), up to 35 hours of battery life with ANC
    off, noise-reduction microphone, memory-foam cushions, USB-C
    charging. Suitable for commuting and office work.
-   **TN-H300 -- Pro Wireless Headphones:** Bluetooth 5.3, hybrid ANC,
    up to 45 hours of battery life with ANC off, high-definition audio
    support, multipoint connectivity for two devices, foldable design,
    USB-C fast charging. Suitable for travel and immersive listening.

### What Is in the Box

Every package includes wireless headphones, a USB-C charging cable, user
manual, and warranty card. TN-H200 and TN-H300 also include a 3.5 mm
audio cable. TN-H300 additionally includes a carrying pouch.

### Charging and Battery

-   Charge using the supplied USB-C cable.
-   A full charge typically takes 2--3 hours, depending on the model.
-   Battery life varies with volume, Bluetooth usage, and ANC settings.
-   A red indicator shows charging; a blue or green indicator indicates
    a full charge, depending on the model.

### Bluetooth Connection Troubleshooting

Ask the customer to:

1.  Ensure the headphones have sufficient battery charge.
2.  Turn on Bluetooth on their phone, tablet, or computer.
3.  Enter pairing mode by holding the power button until the indicator
    flashes.
4.  Select the relevant TN-H100, TN-H200, or TN-H300 device name in
    Bluetooth settings.
5.  If pairing fails, forget the previously saved connection.
6.  Restart both devices and try pairing again.
7.  Test the headphones with another compatible device.

If the issue continues, request the order number, headphone model,
device model, and a description of the problem.

### Problems and What Happens Next

-   **Missing accessory:** Verify package contents and arrange the
    missing part.
-   **Headphones not charging:** Check the cable, power source, and
    charging port.
-   **Bluetooth connection failure:** Complete the pairing
    troubleshooting steps.
-   **Poor sound quality:** Check volume, audio settings, and the
    connected device.
-   **Microphone not working:** Check application permissions and
    microphone selection.
-   **Physical damage:** Escalate to a colleague.
-   **Confirmed manufacturing defect:** Arrange a replacement under the
    warranty policy.

## 2. Smartwatches

### Product Models

-   **TN-W100 -- Essential Smartwatch:** 1.69-inch colour touchscreen,
    Bluetooth connectivity, step and activity tracking, heart-rate
    monitoring, sleep tracking, call and message notifications, up to 7
    days of typical battery life. Suitable for everyday fitness and
    basic smartwatch functions.
-   **TN-W200 -- Active Smartwatch:** 1.85-inch HD touchscreen,
    Bluetooth calling, heart-rate and blood-oxygen monitoring, multiple
    sports modes, sleep and activity tracking, IP68 water resistance, up
    to 10 days of typical battery life. Suitable for active lifestyles.
-   **TN-W300 -- Pro Smartwatch:** 1.95-inch AMOLED touchscreen,
    Bluetooth calling and notifications, activity and workout tracking,
    heart-rate and blood-oxygen monitoring, built-in GPS, multiple
    sports modes, 5 ATM water resistance, up to 12 days of typical
    battery life. Suitable for outdoor activities and advanced
    smartwatch use.

*Health measurements are for general wellness and fitness purposes and
are not a substitute for professional medical advice or diagnostic
equipment.*

### What Is in the Box

Every package includes a smartwatch, magnetic charging cable, standard
watch strap, user manual, and warranty card.

### Charging and Battery

-   Use the supplied magnetic charging cable.
-   Ensure charging contacts are clean and dry.
-   A full charge typically takes 1.5--2.5 hours.
-   Battery life depends on screen brightness, GPS usage, Bluetooth
    calling, and notification frequency.
-   Avoid exposing charging contacts to moisture.

### Pairing and Setup Troubleshooting

Ask the customer to:

1.  Charge the smartwatch before initial setup.
2.  Install the TechNova Fit companion application.
3.  Enable Bluetooth on their smartphone.
4.  Open TechNova Fit and follow the pairing instructions.
5.  Confirm the pairing request on both devices if prompted.
6.  Allow notification and other required permissions.
7.  Restart both devices if pairing fails.
8.  Remove the previous pairing and try again.

The TechNova Fit application supports compatible Android and iOS
devices.

### Problems and What Happens Next

-   **Missing accessory:** Verify package contents and arrange the
    missing item.
-   **Smartwatch not charging:** Clean and align the charging contacts.
-   **Pairing failure:** Follow the pairing troubleshooting procedure.
-   **Display not responding:** Restart the smartwatch.
-   **Notifications not appearing:** Check application permissions and
    notification settings.
-   **Incorrect activity readings:** Check that the watch is worn
    correctly and relevant tracking settings are enabled.
-   **Physical damage:** Escalate to a colleague.
-   **Confirmed manufacturing defect:** Arrange a replacement under the
    warranty policy.

## 3. Bluetooth Speakers

### Product Models

-   **TN-S100 -- Portable Bluetooth Speaker:** Bluetooth 5.2, 10W audio
    output, up to 8 hours of battery life, compact design, built-in
    microphone, USB-C charging. Suitable for personal listening and
    small indoor spaces.
-   **TN-S200 -- Premium Bluetooth Speaker:** Bluetooth 5.3, 20W stereo
    output, up to 12 hours of battery life, enhanced bass, IPX6 water
    resistance, USB-C charging. Suitable for home entertainment and
    outdoor gatherings.
-   **TN-S300 -- Pro Bluetooth Speaker:** Bluetooth 5.3, 40W stereo
    output, up to 18 hours of battery life, enhanced bass and
    high-frequency audio, IP67 dust and water resistance, equalizer
    modes, USB-C fast charging. Suitable for outdoor activities and
    parties.

### What Is in the Box

Every package includes a Bluetooth speaker, USB-C charging cable, user
manual, and warranty card. TN-S300 additionally includes a detachable
carrying strap.

### Charging and Battery

-   Use the supplied USB-C charging cable.
-   A full charge typically takes 2--4 hours, depending on the model.
-   Battery life depends on volume, audio content, and usage conditions.
-   Avoid charging the speaker while its charging port is wet.
-   Ensure the charging port cover is properly closed before exposing a
    water-resistant model to water.

### Bluetooth Connection Troubleshooting

Ask the customer to:

1.  Ensure the speaker has sufficient battery charge.
2.  Turn on the speaker and activate Bluetooth pairing mode.
3.  Enable Bluetooth on the smartphone or computer.
4.  Select the relevant TN-S100, TN-S200, or TN-S300 device name.
5.  If pairing fails, forget the previous connection.
6.  Restart both devices and try pairing again.
7.  Test with another compatible device.

### Problems and What Happens Next

-   **Missing accessory:** Verify package contents and arrange the
    missing part.
-   **Speaker not charging:** Check the cable, power adapter, and
    charging port.
-   **Bluetooth connection failure:** Complete the pairing
    troubleshooting steps.
-   **No sound:** Check volume levels and selected audio output.
-   **Distorted sound:** Reduce volume and test another audio source.
-   **Poor battery performance:** Check volume levels and charging
    behaviour.
-   **Water damage:** Do not charge the device while wet; escalate for
    assessment.
-   **Physical damage:** Escalate to a colleague.
-   **Confirmed manufacturing defect:** Arrange a replacement under the
    warranty policy.
