# TechNova Electronics -- Returns and Warranty Policy

> **Project note:** TechNova Electronics is a fictional company. This
> document adapts the supplied returns and warranty policy for the
> TechNova project. The policy below supersedes the earlier sample
> warranty terms in the product knowledge base.

## Returns

All TechNova orders are placed through Amazon, so returns must also be
handled through Amazon.

-   Customers must start a return from **Your Orders** in their Amazon
    account.
-   The return must be started within **30 days of delivery**.
-   Amazon provides the return label and handles the refund.
-   TechNova cannot start, speed up, or approve a return or refund from
    its side.
-   A human colleague must answer every customer question about refunds.

### What the AI support agent should do

If a customer asks to return a product or requests a refund:

1.  Explain that returns are handled through Amazon.
2.  Direct the customer to **Amazon → Your Orders**.
3.  Remind the customer that the return must be started within 30 days
    of delivery.
4.  Explain that Amazon provides the return label and handles the
    refund.
5.  Do not claim that TechNova has started, approved, or expedited the
    return or refund.
6.  Escalate every refund question to a human colleague.

## Warranty

TechNova products have a **2-year warranty covering manufacturing
faults**.

The warranty applies to manufacturing faults under normal intended use.

The warranty does not cover damage caused by use or accidents, such as
physical damage or punctures.

### Warranty coverage by product category

The following TechNova product categories are covered by the 2-year
manufacturing-fault warranty:

-   **Wireless headphones:** TN-H100, TN-H200, and TN-H300.
-   **Smartwatches:** TN-W100, TN-W200, and TN-W300.
-   **Bluetooth speakers:** TN-S100, TN-S200, and TN-S300.

### Warranty claim information

When a customer reports a possible manufacturing fault, collect:

1.  Amazon order number.
2.  Product model.
3.  Delivery date.
4.  Description of the fault.
5.  Photos or video showing the issue, if useful.

A human colleague should review the claim and confirm whether it
qualifies for warranty support. Do not promise a replacement or other
remedy before that review.
