## Product models and variants

TechNova sells three categories of electronics, with three models in
each category.

### Wireless headphones

-   **TN-H100 -- Essential Wireless Headphones:** Bluetooth 5.2, up to
    20 hours of battery life, built-in microphone, lightweight over-ear
    design, and playback controls.
-   **TN-H200 -- Premium Wireless Headphones:** Bluetooth 5.3, Active
    Noise Cancellation (ANC), up to 35 hours of battery life with ANC
    disabled, noise-reduction microphone, memory-foam cushions, and
    USB-C charging.
-   **TN-H300 -- Pro Wireless Headphones:** Bluetooth 5.3, hybrid ANC,
    up to 45 hours of battery life with ANC disabled, high-definition
    audio support, multipoint connectivity for two devices, foldable
    design, and USB-C fast charging.

### Smartwatches

-   **TN-W100 -- Essential Smartwatch:** 1.69-inch colour touchscreen,
    activity and sleep tracking, heart-rate monitoring, notifications,
    and up to 7 days of typical battery life.
-   **TN-W200 -- Active Smartwatch:** 1.85-inch HD touchscreen,
    Bluetooth calling, heart-rate and blood-oxygen monitoring, sports
    modes, IP68 water resistance, and up to 10 days of typical battery
    life.
-   **TN-W300 -- Pro Smartwatch:** 1.95-inch AMOLED touchscreen,
    Bluetooth calling, activity tracking, heart-rate and blood-oxygen
    monitoring, built-in GPS, 5 ATM water resistance, and up to 12 days
    of typical battery life.

### Bluetooth speakers

-   **TN-S100 -- Portable Bluetooth Speaker:** Bluetooth 5.2, 10W audio
    output, up to 8 hours of battery life, compact design, built-in
    microphone, and USB-C charging.
-   **TN-S200 -- Premium Bluetooth Speaker:** Bluetooth 5.3, 20W stereo
    output, up to 12 hours of battery life, enhanced bass, IPX6 water
    resistance, and USB-C charging.
-   **TN-S300 -- Pro Bluetooth Speaker:** Bluetooth 5.3, 40W stereo
    output, up to 18 hours of battery life, enhanced bass, IP67 dust and
    water resistance, equalizer modes, and USB-C fast charging.

The model code is printed on the product packaging and the product
information label. Customers should provide the full model code when
requesting support.

## Setup and care tips

### General setup

-   Check the model code on the packaging before following
    model-specific instructions.
-   Fully charge the device before first use.
-   Use the charging cable supplied with the product.
-   Keep charging connectors clean and dry.
-   Read the user manual before using product features for the first
    time.
-   Keep the original packaging until the customer has confirmed that
    all included items are present and the device works correctly.

### Wireless headphones

-   Charge using the supplied USB-C cable.
-   To pair, switch the headphones into pairing mode and select the
    matching TN-H model in the phone or computer Bluetooth settings.
-   If pairing fails, remove the saved Bluetooth connection, restart
    both devices, and try again.
-   Battery life depends on volume, usage, and ANC settings.
-   Do not expose the headphones to water unless the specific model
    documentation explicitly confirms water resistance.

### Smartwatches

-   Charge using the supplied magnetic charging cable.
-   Ensure the charging contacts are aligned and dry.
-   Install the TechNova Fit companion application on a compatible
    Android or iOS phone.
-   Pair the watch through the application and allow the permissions
    needed for notifications and other selected features.
-   Battery life depends on screen brightness, GPS use, Bluetooth
    calling, and notification frequency.
-   Water-resistance ratings differ by model. Do not assume that every
    TechNova smartwatch is suitable for swimming.

### Bluetooth speakers

-   Charge using the supplied USB-C cable.
-   Activate pairing mode and select the matching TN-S model in the
    phone or computer Bluetooth settings.
-   If pairing fails, forget the old connection, restart both devices,
    and pair again.
-   Battery life depends on volume and usage conditions.
-   Do not charge a speaker while its charging port is wet. Follow the
    model's water-resistance guidance.

## Problems and what happens next

A colleague handles and confirms every shipment. The AI support agent
must never promise that an item has shipped or give an unconfirmed
delivery date.

-   **Missing charging cable or other accessory:** Confirm the model and
    the relevant accessory part number. Arrange the correct replacement
    accessory after verification.
-   **Missing model-specific extra:** Check the included-accessory list
    for that model and confirm whether the item should have been in the
    box.
-   **Device will not charge:** Ask the customer to check the supplied
    cable, power source, charging port or contacts, and whether the
    charging indicator appears. Do not ask the customer to use a visibly
    damaged cable.
-   **Bluetooth pairing problem:** Follow the troubleshooting steps for
    the relevant product category.
-   **Device has physical damage on arrival:** Request the order number,
    model code, a description of the damage, and clear photos. Escalate
    for review.
-   **One accessory is damaged or missing:** Identify it by its part
    number and arrange the appropriate resolution after verification.
-   **Main device is defective:** Complete the relevant basic
    troubleshooting, collect the required details, and refer the case
    for warranty assessment.
-   **Water exposure:** Do not advise the customer to charge a wet
    device. Check the product's specific water-resistance rating and
    escalate if damage is suspected.
